# 3 — Safety, Evidence, and Authority

Smaug uses a conservative authority model.

```text
input → normalize → validate → assess → authorize → act → record
```

Player and Overtine are both valid input sources. Neither receives authority by
identity alone. Castle/INCLARE remains the authority boundary.

## Firecaster Strain rule

Firecaster becomes eligible at the modeled upper Strain limit when the normal
decision path permits a high-impact proposal. The known-dice comparison is:

```text
known_dice > opposing_known_dice
```

The modeled spellcaster count can contribute to the Good-Odds calculation. The
system records the Good-Odds boundary and participating-player count; neither
becomes authority by itself.

The established Firecaster roll remains:

```text
2D8 + 48 Hit Dice + (Perch Rank × 72 Hit Dice)
```

An Emerald Roll separately adds 96 Hit Dice to Smaug's modeled game state.

`ANGER` may be recorded as simulation context, but it cannot bypass Castle,
INCLARE, provenance, or review. `LIBERTY` means that eligible players remain
open to participation and review under the ordinary game rules.

Administrative simulation gates may require an explicit privilege threshold,
provenance, and protected-path evidence. Firecaster and BreathWeapon are modeled
simulation effects and must never be implemented as destructive filesystem
operations.

Protected files remain intact. Inspection records describe what happened without
silently altering the protected object.

## Human review

Uncertainty, missing provenance, invalid bounds, or policy conflicts route to
review. Habit and historical patterns are advisory and never silently override
current authorization.

The system's IQ terminology is metaphorical engineering vocabulary, not a
psychometric score.

## Canonical sequence

```text
STRAIN → DICE COMPARISON → GOOD-ODDS RECORD
       → CASTLE / INCLARE → SMITE → FIRECASTER → RECORD
```

## Native standards

The gate implementation now fails closed on missing provenance, empty protected
targets, privilege values outside 3–7, invalid stages, and effects outside the
recognized simulation vocabulary. The Herald reports the resulting disposition
but cannot elevate a denied request to authority.

This separation is intentional: authorization is a policy decision; announcing
that decision is an observability operation. Neither operation is a substitute
for the Linux kernel's actual resource and filesystem authority.
